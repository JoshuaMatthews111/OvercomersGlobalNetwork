# Admin Panel - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome! 🎉

You're now an admin for Overcomers Global Network. Here's what you need to know to get started quickly.

---

## First Steps (Do This Today)

### 1. Log In
- Go to: `overcomersglobalnetwork.com/admin`
- Use your email and password

### 2. Explore Your Dashboard
- Check your available features in the left menu
- Look at the dashboard stats
- Notice what you can and cannot access

### 3. Meet the People System
- Click **People** in the left menu (if available)
- This is where you'll manage church contacts
- Try adding a test person to see how it works

---

## Your Main Tools

### 👥 People Management (Most Important)
- **Add new visitors** right after they visit
- **Write notes** after every conversation
- **Tag people** (New Visitor, Member, Leader, etc.)
- **Mark follow-ups** as complete when done

### 📝 Content (If You Have Permission)
- **Blog Posts**: Write teachings and updates
- **Events**: Create church events
- **Content Pages**: Update website information

### 🙏 Community Care
- **Prayer Requests**: Review and respond
- **Enrollments**: Manage discipleship sign-ups
- **Ask The Prophet**: Handle questions

---

## Daily Checklist (5 Minutes)

1. **Dashboard** - Check for new enrollments/bookings
2. **People** - Filter by "Needs Follow-Up" 
3. **Notes** - Add notes for anyone you contacted today

---

## What You CAN'T Do (Master Admin Only)

- Add/remove other admins
- Manage Prophet Joshua's schedule
- Access full donation details
- Change system settings

*If you need these things, ask the Master Admin.*

---

## Need Help?

1. **Check the full Admin Guide** (ADMIN-GUIDE.md)
2. **Ask the Master Admin** for permission changes
3. **Use Notes in People profiles** to communicate with other admins

---

## Pro Tips

- **Add notes immediately** - don't wait until later
- **Use consistent tags** - it helps everyone find people
- **Check "Needs Follow-Up"** daily - don't let people fall through cracks
- **Communicate via Notes** - other admins can see your work

---

## Remember

- We're a team - use the Notes system to keep everyone informed
- Permissions are set by the Master Admin for a reason
- Your work matters - you're helping advance the Kingdom!

Welcome to the team! 🚀
