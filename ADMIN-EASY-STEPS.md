# How to Get to Your Admin Panel
## A Very Simple Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Open Your Web Browser
- First, open your web browser
- This could be Google Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or any browser you use
- Make sure you are connected to the internet

### Step 2: Type the Website Address
- Click on the long white box at the top of your browser
- This is where website addresses go
- Type this exactly: `overcomersglobalnetwork.com/admin`
- Press the Enter key on your keyboard

### Step 3: Wait for the Page to Load
- The page will take a few seconds to load
- You will see a login screen with boxes to type in

### Step 4: Type Your Email
- Click on the first box that says "Email"
- Type your email address very carefully
- Make sure there are no mistakes
- Example: john.smith@email.com

### Step 5: Type Your Password
- Click on the second box that says "Password"
- Type your password exactly as it was given to you
- Passwords are case-sensitive (big letters and small letters matter)
- If you make a mistake, just click in the box again and type over it

### Step 6: Click the Sign In Button
- Look for a button that says "Sign In" or "Login"
- Click this button one time
- Wait for the page to load

### Step 7: You Are Now in Your Admin Dashboard!
- Welcome! You should see your admin dashboard
- This is your main control panel
- On the left side, you will see a menu with different options

---

## What You See on Your Dashboard

### Left Side Menu (Navigation)
- This is how you move around the admin area
- Click on any item to go to that section
- Only items you have permission to use will be clickable

### Main Area (Big Space on Right)
- This is where your work happens
- It changes depending on what you click in the menu

### Your Name at the Top
- You can see your name at the top left
- This confirms you are logged in correctly

---

## How to Use the People Section (Most Important)

### Step 1: Click on "People" in the Left Menu
- Look for the word "People" in the left menu
- Click on it one time
- The page will change to show people management

### Step 2: Add a New Person
- Look for a button that says "Add Person" (usually at the top)
- Click this button
- A small box will pop up asking for information

### Step 3: Fill in the Person's Information
- **First Name**: Type their first name (like "John")
- **Last Name**: Type their last name (like "Smith")
- **Email**: Type their email address (if you have it)
- **Phone**: Type their phone number (if you have it)
- **Address**: Type their address (if you have it)
- **Source**: How did you meet them? (like "Sunday Service" or "Online")
- **Tags**: Type words that describe them (like "New Visitor, Member")

### Step 4: Save the Person
- Click the "Add Person" button at the bottom
- The box will close and the person is now saved

### Step 5: Add Notes About the Person
- Click on the person you just added from the list
- You will see their details on the right
- Look for a box that says "Add a note about this person"
- Type what you talked about with them
- Click the send button (looks like a paper airplane)

---

## How to Add Notes (Very Important)

### Why Add Notes?
- Notes help other admins know what you talked about
- It helps everyone work together as a team
- It reminds you what you discussed last time

### How to Add a Note:
1. Click on the person's name from the list
2. Look for the box that says "Add a note"
3. Type what you want to remember
4. Click the send button
5. Your note is now saved with your name and the time

---

## How to Mark Someone as "Needs Follow-Up"

### Step 1: Find the Person
- Click on "People" in the left menu
- Find the person you need to follow up with
- Click on their name

### Step 2: Look for the Follow-Up Section
- On the right side, look for "Follow-Up Status"
- You will see a button that says "Tag for Follow-Up"
- Click this button

### Step 3: The Person is Now Tagged
- You will see a red dot next to their name
- This means they need follow-up
- Other admins can see this too

### Step 4: Mark Follow-Up as Complete
- After you talk with the person, click "Mark as Followed Up"
- The red dot will disappear
- The system remembers when you did it

---

## How to Find People Who Need Follow-Up

### Step 1: Go to People Section
- Click "People" in the left menu

### Step 2: Look for the "Needs Follow-Up" Button
- At the top, you will see filter buttons
- Find the button that says "Needs Follow-Up"
- Click this button

### Step 3: See Only People Who Need Follow-Up
- Now you only see people with red dots
- This is your daily to-do list
- Contact these people as soon as possible

---

## How to Search for a Person

### Step 1: Go to People Section
- Click "People" in the left menu

### Step 2: Use the Search Box
- At the top, you will see a search box
- It says "Search by name, email, phone..."
- Type the person's name or email
- Press Enter on your keyboard

### Step 3: See the Results
- The list will show only people matching your search
- Click on their name to see their details

---

## How to Log Out (Very Important)

### Step 1: Look for "Sign Out"
- At the bottom of the left menu
- You will see "Sign Out"
- Click on this one time

### Step 2: You Are Now Logged Out
- This keeps your admin area secure
- Always log out when you are finished

---

## Daily Routine (What to Do Each Day)

### Every Morning:
1. Log in to your admin panel
2. Click "People"
3. Click the "Needs Follow-Up" button
4. Call or message the people who need follow-up
5. Mark follow-ups as complete when done
6. Add notes about your conversations
7. Log out when finished

### Every Time Someone Visits Church:
1. Go to "People"
2. Click "Add Person"
3. Fill in their information
4. Add a note about how you met them
5. Tag them as "New Visitor"

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

### Problem: Cannot Log In
- Check your email spelling
- Check your password spelling (big/small letters matter)
- Ask for help if you still cannot log in

### Problem: Cannot See a Feature
- Not all admins have access to all features
- This is normal and okay
- Use the features you do have access to

### Problem: Cannot Find Someone
- Try searching with different spellings
- Try searching by email or phone
- Look through the full list if needed

---

## Remember These Important Things

1. **Always add notes** after talking with people
2. **Always mark follow-ups** as complete when done
3. **Always log out** when you are finished
4. **Ask for help** if you need it
5. **Work as a team** with other admins
